# Provenance boundary

This release candidate contains selected project-controlled Atria work product:

| Area | Recorded origin | Public boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `os` | Atria multi-session and project-governance practice | Generic instructions, templates, and scripts only |
| `compass` | Atria product-direction and requirement practice | Generic method only |
| `design-os` | Atria design workflow and reference-integration guidance | No raw third-party assets, customer cases, Refero archive, or private preference archive |

External products, websites, libraries, and services may be identified by name
or URL for attribution and research reproducibility. Their content is not
relicensed by this repository.

The ignored local `incubator/`, private Refero-derived archive, real project
state, organizational knowledge, credentials, recordings, screenshots, font
binaries, and customer material are outside the distributable boundary.
